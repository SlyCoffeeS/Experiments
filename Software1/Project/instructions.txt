/---Instructions---\

1. Type the number of the option you want to choose.
2. choose exploration if u wanna get started with the game.
3. moving is done by typing (up, down, left, right, or back).
4. Remember to pick up items when entering rooms or defeating slimes. also you can look around to see what is in each room/floor.
5. If you do not have a sword, you will do 5 damage as default. if you pick up a sword you will do 10.
6. You can toss items, but i dont think theres any point in that unless you just dislike items.
7. Remember to save the game if u wanna continue the game later. (Recommended it if you are too busy to finnish a game that takes 2 minutes)

** overall your mission is to explore, collect items and slay the slimes to escape the dungeon and free the world from slimes that want to destroy nature.
